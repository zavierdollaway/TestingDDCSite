Dexterous Design Challenge

The HAND ERC: Dexterous Design Challenge is an 8-Week competition in which multidisciplinary enginering teams of 4-6 people will learn the basics of dexterous robotics and build an 11-DOF hand that will compete in a series of dexterity benchmarks to quantify their success. Winning teams at each schools' local competition will have the opportunity to travel to Texas A&M University for the opportunity to compete in the National Dexterity Olympiad.

HAND ERC

The NSF Human AugmentatioN via Dexterity Engineering Research Center (HAND ERC) aims to revolutionize the ability of robotis to augment human labor by developing easy-to-integrate and verstile dexterous robot hands that make robot manipulators useful "out of the box." These hands will come equipped with a library of AI-powered dexterous skills that grows over time and intuitive interfaces that trained workers can use immediately.
